# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primarily UK visa applicants and job seekers trying to confirm whether a specific employer holds a valid sponsor licence — often mid-application, time-pressured, and unfamiliar with immigration terminology. Secondarily, employers and HR/immigration teams checking their own licence status or a partner/competitor's. Both groups arrive wanting one fast, unambiguous answer rather than a browsing experience.

## Product Purpose

Annaizu Sponsor Licence Checker puts a fast, trustworthy interface over the Home Office's public Register of Licensed Sponsors, so anyone can search and filter sponsors by name, location, type, rating, and visa route without downloading or parsing the raw gov.uk spreadsheet. Success is a user finding and understanding a sponsor's status within seconds, understanding what "rating" actually means, and trusting that they're looking at real, current government data.

## Positioning

The fastest, clearest way to check whether a UK employer is a licensed visa sponsor — built directly on the government's own published register, not a jobs board dressed up as one.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, clear, modern-professional, and deliberately not gimmicky. The emotional job is calm confidence: visa-related searches are often anxious moments, and the interface should lower that anxiety through clarity and responsiveness rather than reassurance copy. Motion and polish should read as competence, not entertainment.

## Anti-references

Explicitly avoid the generic UK govtech look — dated bureaucratic forms, cramped tables, heavy borders, low-contrast grey-on-grey portals. This should feel like a well-built modern product that happens to be built on official data, not an official-feeling data dump.

## Design Principles

- Government data, product-grade execution: the interactivity and polish exist to build trust and speed comprehension, never to distract from the data.
- Instant feedback: search and filters must feel live — no full-page reloads or spinner-gated interactions for a basic query.
- Plain-language clarity, no legal framing: explain sponsor licences and ratings factually and in plain English; never drift into legal advice.
- Motion with restraint: scroll reveals, hover states, and counters serve comprehension and delight in equal, disciplined measure — never spectacle for its own sake — and every animation has a reduced-motion alternative.
- One dataset, no scope creep: the product's entire job is search, filter, and explain. No jobs board, no listings, no application flow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as the bar: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large/bold text ≥3:1, full keyboard operability and visible focus states across search, filters, pagination, and the sponsor detail view, semantic landmarks and ARIA labelling for filter controls and live result counts, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every scroll/hover/counter animation.
